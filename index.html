Questions

Good to know.

Are the uploaded videos private?

Yes. The tool sets every uploaded video's visibility to private. View it in YouTube Studio while signed in to the authorized channel.

Will rerunning a folder upload everything again?

Usually no. Successful uploads are recorded locally and matching file contents are skipped under the same OAuth grant. If you reset history or reauthorize uploads, check YouTube Studio before rerunning older files.

Do I need to use a playlist?

No. Choose “No playlist” in the menu or run a direct upload without playlist options. Playlist access is optional and asks for a separate Google permission.

Where does the upload history live?

In a local SQLite database in your operating system's application data folder. You can view it in the CLI or export a Markdown table. No video files are stored in the database.

Is this a backup service?

No. Keep the original files and a separate backup for anything irreplaceable. YouTube may process uploaded videos, and availability is subject to your account and YouTube policies.
