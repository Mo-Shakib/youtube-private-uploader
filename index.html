How it works

Three steps to your first private upload.

1

Connect your channel

Create a Google Cloud Desktop app OAuth client and enable YouTube Data API v3. The setup guide walks you through it.

2

Choose your videos

Launch the menu with python3 ytupload.py, or pass a video or folder path directly.

3

Upload and organize

Authorize the intended channel, choose a playlist if useful, and follow progress in the terminal.

Common questions

Good to know before you start.

Are uploads really private?

Yes. The tool sets each uploaded video's visibility to private. You can view it in YouTube Studio while signed in to the authorized channel.

Will rerunning a folder upload everything again?

Usually no. Successful uploads are stored in local history and matching file contents are skipped under the same OAuth grant. If you reset history or reauthorize uploads, check YouTube Studio before rerunning older files.

Do I need a playlist?

No. Choose “No playlist” in the menu, or run a direct upload command without a playlist option. Playlist access is optional and asks for a separate Google permission.

Where is my history stored?

In a local SQLite database in your operating system's application data folder. You can view it in the CLI or export a Markdown table. The tool does not store video files in the database.

Is this a backup service?

No. Keep the original files and a separate backup for anything you cannot replace. YouTube may process uploaded videos, and availability is subject to your account and YouTube policies.
